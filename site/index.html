Name the playbook. That is the part that matters.

In the release evals, on a plain bug report, no session produced the bug-fix playbook’s step list on its own (0 of 3, with or without the plugin). Naming the playbook is the reliable path.

Copy this prompt and swap in your own problem. It names the playbook, says what happens first, and gives a done check that can fail.
