### Autopilot-full

**You own the verdicts, never the PRs. One owner carries each PR to merge, and nothing merges until a verifier that never wrote it has proven the code that lands.** For independent PRs and your human partner's grant, in their own words, to merge once verified. Without that grant, run `playbooks/autopilot-stack.md`.

1. **Record the grant first.** Quote it as the trail's first row (the `show-me-your-work` skill). That row is the merge approval the `finishing-a-development-branch` skill asks for. It covers only the queue's items, and the Always-pause list in `SKILL.md` Autonomy still holds. It also covers each owner force-pushing its own PR branch with `--force-with-lease`, the one exception to `playbooks/babysit.md` step 4; say so when you quote it. PRs outside the queue, or kept by your human partner, stop at merge-ready.
2. **Spawn one owner per PR, in parallel, each off `main`.** An owner is a `zed` subagent in its own worktree (the `using-git-worktrees` skill), modeled per the `subagent-driven-development` skill's Model Selection. The brief names the playbooks it follows; `zed`'s skip of the router does not cover them. It opens its PR early with `gh pr create` and the PR body rules of `playbooks/opening-a-pr.md`; you run that playbook's `comment-sicko` pass, and the grant row answers the `finishing-a-development-branch` hand-off, because an owner can neither dispatch nor ask. It proves the change on the real surface, and babysits per `playbooks/babysit.md`. At code-ready it rebases onto `main`, runs the touched paths' checks, and reports the head. It reports every later head, then merge-ready.
3. **Assume the session dies.** Owners push early and often. Log each owner's branch, PR, agent id, and expected runtime in the trail, so `playbooks/session-pickup.md` can replace owners after a crash. An owner force-pushes only its own branch, with `--force-with-lease` at the SHA it last pushed, and stops on any push it did not make.
4. **Verify once, at the code-ready head.** Fan out verifiers (the `dispatching-parallel-agents` skill) that never wrote the code, on a different model. Lanes rerun the gates, prove the behavior live on the user's surface, and audit the diff and the owner's receipts. No verdict is clean without the live lane. Only new behavior or a large new diff earns another full pass. Otherwise one verifier checks the new head, and the patch-id rule in `playbooks/shipping.md` step 3 can carry a lane.
5. **Fix forward through the owner.** Send all proven findings in one batch, with a failing test at every site sharing the defect. A defect `main` already has does not gate the PR. Never push to an owner's branch.
6. **Merge only what was verified on the `main` it lands on.** Before merging, the owner fetches `main`. A conflict, a file both sides changed, or a change to which checks run means rebase and wait for CI. A change to code the patch calls means run its checks on the merge result. Otherwise merge and read it back per `playbooks/shipping.md` step 4. A third rebase holds for you to sequence.
7. **Countersign anything that loosens a gate.** A raised budget, an allowlist entry, or a skipped check needs a verifier's proof and your countersign in the trail, because the owner wants green. A disabled check always comes back out. Never give or bypass an approval the forge enforces.
8. **Audit on every wake** (`playbooks/autonomous-run.md` step 2) until the queue drains. Re-read this playbook, probe owners by pushes and PR activity rather than status text, and replace a stuck one at once with a fresh owner given its brief, directives, and branch. Stuck means erroring or silent past its expected runtime. An owner waiting on you is not stuck, so answer it.
9. **Stand down on a stop.** Every owner gets a zero-writes order, reports where it stands, and keeps its brief.
